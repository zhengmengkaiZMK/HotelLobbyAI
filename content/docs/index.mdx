## What is ViralReel?

ViralReel (https://www.viralreel.app) is a template-first AI video site for short-form trends. You pick a format, upload the photos that template asks for, and export an MP4. You do not write a prompt.

There are no free credits and no free trial. Every render is paid. The generator button shows the exact credit cost before you start. A default 15-second clip is 60 credits at 480p, 75 credits at 720p (about $6.25 at 12 credits = $1), and 90 credits at 1080p. Longer templates cost more. Packs start at $9.9 for 90 credits on the pricing page. Monthly plans start at $9.9/month on the subscribe page.

Failed or canceled generations return the credits for that job.
