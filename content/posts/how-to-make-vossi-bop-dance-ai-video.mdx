## Step 2 — Upload Your Photo

The generator shows one upload slot labeled "Your photo". The example image inside the slot — the dancer from the Angry Ginge clip — shows the target: one person, full body, clearly lit, standing free of crowds.

What works best:

- Full-body shot, from head to feet. The Vossi Bop moves the whole body, and a cropped torso bops badly.
- Good lighting and sharp focus, so the AI can keep the face, hair and outfit recognizable.
- A plain or simple background. Crowds, pets and busy rooms confuse the subject detection.
- No sunglasses covering the face, no heavy filters.

## Step 3 — Choose Your Video Settings

## Tips for Better AI Dance Results

- Use a clear, sharp photo — AI cannot invent detail that a blurry photo never had.
- Keep the subject visible from head to feet.
- Avoid blurry or heavily edited images; heavy filters distort faces during motion.
- Follow the template's image requirements (one person, full body, simple background).
- Review the generated video before sharing — if the face drifts, try a more front-facing photo.
