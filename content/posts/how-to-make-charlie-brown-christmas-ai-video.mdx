The **A Charlie Brown Christmas** trend is the easiest AI format of the season to make well, because the input is one photo and the whole craft is choosing the *right* photo. This guide covers the fastest browser route, the photo rules that decide whether the cartoon looks like you, and how to post it the way the trend's biggest posts do.

## What you need

- One clear photo of yourself (or someone who agreed, or your pet)
- A browser — no app, no CapCut account, no prompt to write
- A few minutes for the render

## Step 2 — Upload the right photo

This step decides everything. The redraw keeps exactly what a clear photo gives it:

- **Waist-up or full body**, face to the camera
- **Bright, even light** — the flat 1965 style needs clean shapes
- **Hair, glasses, and outfit fully in frame**, with the colors and prints readable
- **No sunglasses, no masks** — the dot eyes sit under your own glasses, which is the single most recognizable thing in the trend
- **No costume photos** — or the AI may redraw you into the wrong trend
- **Pets:** full body, sitting or standing, fur pattern visible. A headshot of a black cat comes back as a grey blob.

Do not upload minors, celebrities, or screenshots of the 1965 special.

## Step 4 — Post it like the trend does

1. **Caption the countdown.** The biggest posts say nothing but the number of days left until Christmas — *"62"*, *"Sixty days till Christmas!"*. Update the number every morning and the format gives you a fresh post daily.
2. **Add the hashtags.** `#charliebrownchristmas` and `#snoopyfilter` are the two the trend's tutorials use.
3. **Add the sound in the app.** The trend audio is the *original sound* from the first @jason_l_smith post — find it on the trend's biggest TikToks or in your app's library. ViralReel does not license it or the Vince Guaraldi score.
4. **Label it AI.** TikTok and Instagram both offer an AI-generated tag. The trend's biggest accounts keep it on.
5. **Time it.** December is the window — the trend started when the countdown became postable, and "snoopy filter" searches peak in November.

## Common mistakes

- **Sunglasses or a mask.** The style keeps your glasses as simple bold frames; sunglasses delete the joke.
- **A dim, blurry photo.** Flat colors need clean edges; blur becomes mush.
- **A cropped half-face.** The cartoon lands full-body on the doghouse — head-to-shoe detail makes it read.
- **Group photo in a one-photo slot.** One photo renders one cartoon. For couples and families, render one person per pass.
- **Posting without the AI label.** The whole trend is openly AI; keeping the label keeps it fun.
