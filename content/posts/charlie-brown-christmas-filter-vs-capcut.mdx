The **Charlie Brown Christmas filter** spread on TikTok through a **CapCut template named "A Charlie Brown"** — that is where the first post came from, and where most posts still come from. But it is not the only route. ViralReel runs the same scan-line redraw in the browser with an image-to-video model. Both are fan work, neither is official Peanuts, and which one fits your post depends on what you are posting.

## The two routes at a glance

| | CapCut template "A Charlie Brown" | ViralReel browser template |
|---|---|---|
| **Where** | Inside the CapCut app | Any browser |
| **Input** | One photo; group photos up to 4 people | One photo per render |
| **How it works** | Your photo is dropped into the template's fixed scene at a fixed size | The model runs the move itself — title, scan line, doghouse — on your photo |

## When CapCut is the right pick

- **Group shots.** The template takes up to four people from one photo and gives everyone their own cartoon, in the order they stood. That is the version couples and families post.
- **Sound baked in.** The template's export carries the trend audio, so the post is ready to ship inside one app.
- **Zero budget.** The template itself is free.

## When the browser route wins

- **A cleaner single-subject redraw.** With one clear photo and no scene-compositing, the model has the whole frame for your face, glasses, and outfit — the details the joke depends on.
- **The 9:16 size.** The ViralReel render is 9:16, the ratio TikTok, Reels, and Shorts use — no crop, no letterboxing.
- **No app install.** Phone storage is not infinite.
- **Watermark-free MP4 on paid plans**, with failed jobs refunded.

## What both routes share

- **The look.** The 1965 *A Charlie Brown Christmas* cel style: flat colors, thick outlines, dot eyes, the red doghouse with the wreath, the hand-lettered title.
- **The caption format.** The days left until Christmas — nothing else.
- **The hashtags.** `#charliebrownchristmas` and `#snoopyfilter`.
- **The rules.** Fan work only — Peanuts Worldwide and the Schulz estate own the characters. Use photos you have permission to use, skip minors and celebrities, and keep the AI-generated label on.
- **The sound caveat.** The trend sound is the *original sound* from @jason_l_smith's first post — a Christmas track, not the Vince Guaraldi score. On the browser route you add it in the app you post to. More in [the song and the history](/blog/charlie-brown-christmas-song-and-history).

## The verdict

Posting as a couple or a family tonight, with the sound already in the file? CapCut. Posting your own countdown — or your dog's — and want the sharpest redraw in 9:16 without installing anything? The browser route. Most creators end up using both: CapCut for group shots, the browser for their own daily countdown.
