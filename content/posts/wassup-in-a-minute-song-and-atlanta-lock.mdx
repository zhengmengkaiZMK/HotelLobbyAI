## The song: a "From Time" jersey-club remix

The wassup in a minute sound is a jersey-club remix built on **"From Time"**, the 2013 track by Drake featuring Jhené Aiko. The remix had been circulating on TikTok since January 2026, but in late October 2026 it collided with a new animation style and became the trend sound.

The name-giving moment comes early in the remix: the vocal says **"wassup, been a minute"** — a greeting. Most listeners hear "wassup **in** a minute", and that mishearing became the trend's name and hashtag, #wassupinaminute. ## The style: what Atlanta Lock means

**Atlanta Lock** is a fan nickname for drawings of the *Blue Lock* cast as Atlanta rappers: glossy puffer jackets, gold chains, baggy jeans, and — in the original trend edit — a fanned stack of banknotes in one hand. The name plays on the anime's title, and the style has existed in fan art for years before the trend.

On 22 October 2026 the US animator @ristyisvalid animated several of those streetwear drawings swaying side to side on a white background; the follow-up captioned "Wassup in a minute" named the trend. Within days, remakes with other anime characters and AI tutorials filled the tag.
