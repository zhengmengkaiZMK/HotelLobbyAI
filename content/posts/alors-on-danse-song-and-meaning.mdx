## What "Alors on danse" means

The title is French for **"so we dance"**. The song's verses pile up the weight of ordinary life — work stress, bills, debt, burnout — and each refrain answers with the same shrug: *alors on danse* — so we dance. That contrast between heavy verses and an irresistible synth hook is exactly why the song never aged: the joke and the groove are the same thing.

It also explains the 2026 trend's comedy. In the sketch, one man carries all the chaos and one man sits untouched at the keyboard — and the song plays over both, telling everyone to dance anyway.

## The song behind the meme

- **Written and performed by Stromae** — the stage name of Paul Van Haver, a Belgian artist with a Rwandan father and a Flemish mother. The name is verlan (French slang that flips syllables) for *Maestro*.
- **Released in 2009** and a phenomenon across Europe: the track topped charts across the continent and turned the then-unknown producer into a star almost overnight.
- **A key detail fans love** — Stromae wrote the hit on his own, which is the exact opposite of the sketch, where the songwriting looks like a chaotic Jamel Debbouze production.
- **2010 — the Kanye West remix** gave the track a second life in the English-speaking world and cemented it as a global reference.
- **The sketch** aired in 2010 on *Made in Jamel*, Jamel Debbouze's variety show: Jamel storms Stromae's studio, dances, points at the camera, and clowns around while Stromae deadpans at a mini keyboard. That clip is the scene the 2026 AI trend recreates.

## Three memes, one song — do not mix them up

Because the song is so recognizable, its name has collected three different memes:

1. **The 2010 sketch** — Jamel and Stromae in the white studio. The source material of the 2026 AI trend.
2. **The 2021 group-dance trend** — slow-motion crowds strutting in sync to the track. A dance edit, no sketch, no AI.
3. **The 2026 AI reswap** — two new faces dropped into the sketch by photo-to-video AI. This is the current trend, and it needs exactly two photos.

If the video has a white room and a mini keyboard, it is meme 1 or 3. If it is a crowd strutting outdoors, it is 2021. The full timeline of the 2026 wave is in [what is Alors On Danse AI](/blog/what-is-alors-on-danse-ai).
