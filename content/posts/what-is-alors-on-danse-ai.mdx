**Alors On Danse AI** is a TikTok trend from October 2026: AI-generated videos that swap two new faces into the famous 2010 white-studio sketch from Jamel Debbouze's variety show *Made in Jamel*. In the original scene, Jamel storms into Stromae's studio, dances, points at the camera and goofs around — while Stromae, the Belgian artist behind the 2009 smash hit "Alors on danse", sits deadpan at a tiny keyboard, utterly unimpressed. The AI trend puts you and a friend in those two chairs: one photos-bomber dancing, one long-suffering keyboard player.

The name comes from the song itself — French for "so we dance" — and this page covers what the trend looks like, the full timeline of how it came back in 2026, who is who in the sketch, and how to make your own version with the [Alors On Danse AI template](/alors-on-danse-ai-template).

## What an Alors On Danse AI video looks like

Every post in the trend shares the same skeleton:

1. **The white studio.** A bright, seamless white set with a couple of props — the unmistakable look of the 2010 TV sketch.
2. **The two roles.** One person is seated at the small keyboard, playing with a straight face. The other bursts in and dances — pointing at the camera, messing around, stealing the scene. The comedy is the contrast: one tries, one does not care.
3. **The swap.** The faces are AI-generated from two uploaded photos, so the dancing goofball and the deadpan keyboardist are whoever the poster chose — F1 drivers, footballers, fantasy characters, or you and a friend.
4. **The sound.** The original 2009 track "Alors on danse" — its synth hook is what makes the scene instantly recognizable.
5. **The honesty badge.** The TikTok AI label sits in the corner. The sketch belongs to Jamel Debbouze and Stromae; fan parodies keep the AI label on and never pretend the original actors are in the clip.

## Who is who in the sketch

- **Jamel Debbouze** — the French-Moroccan comedian and actor who hosted the variety show *Made in Jamel*. In the sketch he is the one dancing: bursting into the studio, pointing at the camera, playing to the audience.
- **Stromae** — the Belgian musician (real name Paul Van Haver) who wrote and performs "Alors on danse". In the sketch he plays himself at the mini keyboard, keeping a famously straight face while Jamel clowns around.
- **The joke behind the joke** — the sketch parodies the songwriting process as if it were this chaotic duet, when in reality Stromae wrote the hit on his own.

## Why the format works

1. **The scene is fixed and famous** — sixteen years of people recognize the white studio in one second.
2. **Two roles, one variable** — the sketch only needs two faces, which is exactly what photo-to-video AI handles best.
3. **The contrast carries the comedy** — the dancer tries hard, the keyboardist does not care, and that joke survives any face swap.
4. **The song does the branding** — the synth hook files every clip under the meme automatically.
