## Fixed template, prompt tools, or face-paste editors?

Three kinds of tools will answer a "Gangsta's Paradise AI video generator" search, and they are not interchangeable:

| Mode | How it works | What you give up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fixed browser template (ViralReel)** | Five staged performances; two photo slots; the render mirrors the reference performance | No custom scenes — the five takes are the catalog |
| **Blank prompt tools** | You describe the scene to an image-to-video model and steer it with text | Every render is a gamble; staging, choir payoff and shot grammar are yours to invent each time |
| **Face-paste editors** | A fixed clip with a face pasted onto one performer | One static face swap, not two re-rendered performers walking and singing through the scene |

If the goal is the meme as it circulates — verse, walk-past, choir payoff — the fixed template is the shortest path. If you want a scene nobody has staged yet, a prompt tool is the honest choice, with the hit rate that implies.

## FAQ

**Do I need an account to generate?**
You need an account so the job has a credit balance and a generation history; there are no free credits and no free trial. The generator itself runs in the browser — nothing to install.

**Can I use the video commercially?**
The template stages an original performance in the style of the meme; it does not include the song or claim the real artists. **What photos work best?**
Sharp, evenly lit, front-facing for the rapper; head fully visible, waist-up or full-body for the singer; 300×300 pixels minimum. Heavy filters, sunglasses and extreme angles all cost face accuracy.

**Why is the ratio locked to auto?**
It is not locked — auto is the default because the staged reference clips are 4:3 landscape, and the output follows their framing. You can pick 9:16 or 16:9 if the platform you post to needs it.

**What happens if a render fails?**
The credits for that job are returned automatically. Re-running the same two photos usually lands cleaner; if faces drift, sharpen the source photos before retrying.
