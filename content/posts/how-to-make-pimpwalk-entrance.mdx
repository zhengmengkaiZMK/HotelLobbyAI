## Step 2 — Upload two full-body photos, walker first

This is the whole craft:

- **Photo 1** is whoever should walk through the door. **Photo 2** is the friend who pulls it open and grooves beside it.
- **Full body, one person per photo.** The joke is in the legs — the low, leaned-back strut and the near-crouch — so a selfie crop leaves the model guessing.
- **Door-holder needs attitude too.** The hype man grooves in place the whole time, so a photo with some swing in it reads better than a stiff passport shot.
- **The guest does not need to fit the doorway.** That is the joke. A dad in cargo shorts, a boss in a puffer, a cat — the bigger the mismatch with the dandy entrance, the better it lands.
- One clear face per photo; with two faces in one photo, the model picks who struts.

## Troubleshooting

- **No crouch, just walking** — photo was not full-body, or the legs were hidden. Retry with the full silhouette visible.
- **The wrong person holds the door** — photo order swapped. Photo 1 walks in, photo 2 holds the door.
- **The doorway changes style** — that is the cast, not a bug. Each cast holds its own shop front.
