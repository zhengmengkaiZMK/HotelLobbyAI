## The photo the scan line can use

The generator does not ask you to describe the doghouse. The clip already has the title, the scan line, and the snow. Your file only has to be a person or a pet the model can lock onto.

1. **Face the camera.** A slight angle is fine. A profile, or the back of a head, gives the cartoon almost nothing to keep.
2. **Keep real glasses in frame.** The dot eyes sit under your own frames. That is the detail people recognize. Sunglasses hide it. A mask hides the mouth and the cheeks.
3. **Waist-up, or full body.** The finished cartoon stands on the doghouse from head to feet. A tight headshot can still run. It gives the pose less of you to hang on.
4. **Hair and outfit in frame.** The redraw keeps hairstyle, facial hair, and the colors and prints of the clothes as flat shapes. A hat you want to keep has to be in the photo.
5. **Even light in front.** A window behind the person turns them into a silhouette, and the flat colors have nowhere clean to sit.
6. **One subject.** A group selfie makes the model guess which face to draw. Crop to one person, or render each adult who agreed as a separate pass.
