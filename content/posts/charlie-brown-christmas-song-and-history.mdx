## The score people expect: Vince Guaraldi, 1965

The music everyone *associates* with A Charlie Brown Christmas is different: **"Christmas Time Is Here"** and **"Linus and Lucy"**, composed by **Vince Guaraldi** for the TV special that first aired on **CBS on 9 December 1965**. It was a surprising choice for a kids' show — jazz, with a children's choir — and it became inseparable from the holiday itself. The special's hand-lettered title card and Snoopy's decorated red doghouse are the other two things the filter borrows.

## The 1965 special in one paragraph

*A Charlie Brown Christmas* first aired on CBS on 9 December 1965, directed by Bill Melendez from Charles M. Schulz's Peanuts comic strip. It looked unlike anything on television — flat blocks of color, no shading, thick hand-drawn outlines, actual children voicing the characters — and it carried a grown-up message about commercialism that helped it become an annual tradition for six decades. The scene the trend borrows is Snoopy decorating his red doghouse with lights and a wreath for the Christmas play. The special and the characters belong to **Peanuts Worldwide and the Charles M. Schulz estate**; the 2026 filter trend is fan work end to end.
