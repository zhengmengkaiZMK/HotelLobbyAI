Search **NostalgIA AI** today and you get two completely different memes fighting over one word. This page separates them, because knowing which one you are looking at is the difference between finding a song and finding the [retro TV-ad video format](/nostalgia-ai-template) everyone is posting on TikTok.

## The word itself: nostalgia + IA

**IA** is how Spanish and Portuguese speakers abbreviate *inteligencia artificial* — artificial intelligence. Weld it to **nostalgia** and you get *NostalgIA*: a word that literally reads as "AI nostalgia." The spelling is the joke doing its own marketing — the trend it names is exactly that, AI-generated videos that romanticize the past, in this case the golden age of television commercials.

## Meaning #1: the 2023 AI song (the old NostalgIA)

In October 2023, the Chilean producer Mauricio Bustos — working as **FlowGPT** — released "NostalgIA (DEMO 5)," a reggaeton track built with AI voices imitating Bad Bunny, Daddy Yankee, and Justin Bieber. It went massively viral on TikTok, Bad Bunny publicly told fans who liked it to leave his WhatsApp channel, FlowGPT replied as the "AI entity," and the controversy became a meme of its own. It is covered on Know Your Meme as the Bad Bunny AI Song "NostalgIA" Controversy.

That song is why the word had keyword history when the 2026 trend arrived — and why search results about "NostalgIA" still return music stories. It has **nothing to do with the video format**.

## Meaning #2: the 2026 TikTok trend (the one with CapCut in the name)

The current **NostalgIA** is a late-October 2026 TikTok format: AI videos styled as **recreated retro TV commercials**. A letterboxed standard-definition frame, a sunny suburban backyard, a chocolate crisp bar, one bite, a straight-to-camera pitch with an over-the-top ad smile, and a red jingle text card — captioned **"pov: you always wanted to know how you'd look in this ad."** The ad never existed; it is a shared-memory parody of classic candy-bar TV spots. That is the trend people mean by "NostalgIA CapCut" or "NostalgIA AI" in 2026.

The full story of the format is in [What is NostalgIA CapCut](/blog/what-is-nostalgia-capcut).
