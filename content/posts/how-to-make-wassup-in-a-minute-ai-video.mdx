The wassup in a minute trend is simple to recognize and simple to join: a streetwear anime character on a white background, swaying side to side to a jersey-club remix, fifteen seconds, vertical. If you have seen the edits and want your own character in that sway line, this is the exact workflow — no drawing, no keyframes, no prompt writing.

## What you are making

A wassup in a minute AI video on [ViralReel](/) is a new 9:16 animation generated from your photos: you (and up to three friends) drawn as original streetwear anime characters — the Atlanta Lock style — swaying on the beat against a pure white background with a soft moving shadow. It is a new fictional animation, not the original TikTok edit and not a fan artist's drawing, which is what makes it safe to post with the AI label on.

Not sure what the trend is first? Read [What is wassup in a minute](/blog/what-is-wassup-in-a-minute).

## Step by step

1. **Open the template.** Go to the [Wassup in a Minute AI template](/wassup-in-a-minute-ai-template). The white background, the streetwear outfits, and the sway are all pre-built.
2. **Upload one to four photos.** One person per photo — the template puts up to four characters in the row, like the original edit. Use clear, front-facing photos of yourself or people who agreed to it; skip sunglasses and heavy filters, because the face carries the drawing.
3. **Pick an outfit.** Five variants ship with the template: Money Fan (the fanned stack of banknotes from the original), Puffer Classic, All Black, Jersey Look, and Heavyweight. The prompt is pre-filled for each and stays editable — keep the white background and the sway so the result still reads as this trend.
4. **Keep 9:16 and 15 seconds.** The template fixes the length at 15s so the sway lands on the beat, and the vertical ratio is what TikTok, Reels and Shorts use for this format. Read the credit cost the generator shows, then generate.
5. **Download the MP4** as soon as the render finishes.
6. **Add the sound, label it AI, post natively.** In TikTok, Reels or Shorts, search "wassup in a minute" (or "wassup been a minute") in the sound picker and pick the top trend sound. Start your clip on the first beat so your sway lands on "wassup". Use #wassupinaminute plus #animation and #atlantalock, keep the AI-generated label on, and post directly from the app — cross-posting tools add watermarks and throttle reach.

## Three tips that separate good posts from scroll-past ones

- **Make one for each friend and post them back to back.** The original edit is a *row* of characters — a sequence of clips with different friends recreates that on your profile.
- **Batch your renders.** Generate a few outfit variations and pick the best two or three; a run of clips beats one "perfect" one on a trend this new.
- **Never upload what you do not have rights to.** No minors, no celebrities, no strangers grabbed from the original edit, and no *Blue Lock* characters — the [AI version works precisely because it draws you](/wassup-in-a-minute-blue-lock) instead of a show character.
