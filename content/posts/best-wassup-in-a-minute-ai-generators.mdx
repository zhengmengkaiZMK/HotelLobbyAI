## What you are actually making

The trend is streetwear anime characters swaying on white. Fans nicknamed that look Atlanta Lock. The sound is a jersey-club remix of Drake’s “From Time” with Jhené Aiko. The lyric is “wassup, been a minute,” also heard as “wassup in a minute,” “wassup wait a minute,” or “wasala.” “In a minute” is that lyric. It is not a one-minute render and not the clip length. ViralReel’s generator says a video usually takes 2–3 minutes. The export is 15 seconds.

One photo is one character. It is not the original four-character rotation. ViralReel lines up one to four adults, one per photo. Jump can put more than one person in a single frame. angles is four poses of the same person. A reference track, a take, or a demo beat is not a license of the remix. ViralReel does not export it. The song essay is [the song and the Atlanta Lock style](https://www.viralreel.app/blog/wassup-in-a-minute-song-and-atlanta-lock).
