This comparison breaks down what each tool actually does, what it costs to produce trend-style videos on each platform, and which one fits your workflow. Pricing is from each company's own pricing page as of October 2026.

## LightX: great editor, generic video

LightX is an impressive all-in-one workspace: photo retouching, avatars, logo design, product photos, and an AI video generator that turns text or images into motion. If your main job is editing photos, it is a reasonable tool at a low price — the yearly Pro plan runs about SGD 6.41 per month with 1,000 AI credits.

The catch for trend content: LightX's video generator is a general-purpose image-to-video tool. There is no dedicated template for the duo booth format, no pet editions, and no lip sync tuned to the trend audio. To approximate the orange booth look you would write prompts, generate, review, and regenerate — and every attempt consumes credits from the same pool you use for photo editing. You can get there eventually; it is just a different, slower path than picking a finished template.

## Everygen: the model buffet

Everygen (formerly Viewmax) aggregates the newest video models — Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0 Turbo, Veo 3.1, MiniMax — and adds marketing tooling like auto-captions, voiceovers, and an MCP integration that lets Claude drive video creation. Its plans are sized in outputs: the Starter tier covers about 18 Seedance 2 Fast videos per month, mid tiers 38, and the top tier about 76 videos plus a large media allowance.

This is a genuine power platform. But flexibility has a cost: you write every prompt, pick every model, and iterate on every render. None of the models is tuned for the specific two-person trend format, so matching the recognizable booth composition, the lip sync, and the framing takes prompt skill and several attempts. If you love experimenting, Everygen is fun. If you want the same viral format your audience already recognizes, it is the long way around.

## Higgsfield: pro suite, premium burn

Higgsfield is the most ambitious of the three competitors: an Ads Studio for recreating ads with your products, an AI Influencer studio, cinematic effects presets, and a film-festival-grade community. Plans run $15/mo (200 credits), $39/mo (1,000 credits), and $99/mo (3,000 credits).

The credit economics matter here. Video costs depend on the model: Kling 3.0 at 720p runs about 7 credits per 5-second clip, while Seedance 2.0 at 720p runs about 22 credits per 5 seconds — and premium models like Veo 3.1 cost far more. For cinematic short films and ad visuals, that pricing is competitive for what you get. For trend videos, where you typically want 8–15 second clips and several variations, the credits add up quickly and you carry the full prompt-engineering burden yourself.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is ViralReel cheaper than Higgsfield?**
For trend-format videos, yes in practice. ViralReel's fixed costs (6–18 credits per video) mean the $19.9 Plus plan covers roughly 13–25 finished videos. On Higgsfield, comparable 8-second clips at Kling 720p cost about 11 credits each, pushing 20 videos past the Starter plan — and premium models cost several times more.

**Can LightX or Everygen make the Hotel Lobby booth video?**
Not as a template. They can generate generic video from images and prompts, but the booth composition, lip sync, and trend framing all depend on your prompts, with no guarantee of consistency across attempts.

**Do all of these tools include the trend audio?**
ViralReel renders lip sync tuned to the trend beat; you add the official sound in TikTok/Reels when posting, which also helps the algorithm group you with the trend. The general-purpose tools do not include trend-specific audio handling.

**What if a generation fails?**
On ViralReel, failed or canceled jobs are refunded to your credit balance automatically. General-purpose platforms typically do not refund a prompt that produced an unusable result.
