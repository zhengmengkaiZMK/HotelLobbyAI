## What the camera should see

Even light on the face matters more than a clever background. The template replaces the room with white.

- Face the camera. A slight angle is fine. A profile, or the back of a head, gives the drawing almost nothing to lock onto.
- Put the light in front of the person. A window behind them turns the face into a silhouette.
- Keep both eyes visible. Heavy sunglasses, a hand over the mouth, or hair that covers the face make the character harder to read.
- Include the head and some of the body. Knees-up or full body is enough. A tiny face in a wide crowd shot is not.
- Skip beauty filters and screenshots of a screen. A photo of a photo adds a second frame the model has to fight.
- JPG, PNG, and WebP are the formats the how-to already names. The minimum the generator states is 240×240.
