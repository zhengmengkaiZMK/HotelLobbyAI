## Photo slots: Left Dancer, Right Dancer

The generator shows two buttons:

1. **Left Dancer** — the photo on the left of the frame.
2. **Right Dancer** — the photo on the right of the frame.

Use a full-body, front-facing photo for each dancer. Head-to-toe photos track the choreography better than close-ups. The buttons are not labeled solo or sync, and the render does not promise which photo dances which eight-count. The routine comes from the take you picked.

Two photos give the cleanest duo. One photo also works: the template cast fills the other dancer until you upload a second photo. For three or four people, keep the duo structure and let extras stand in one of the photos. Do not upload more photos than the generator shows slots for. Use photos you have permission to use. Do not upload minors without consent, celebrities, or screenshots of the original TikTok.
