## What the Dance with Santa video looks like

Before you upload anything, know the target: a wood-panelled Christmas hall with a red carpet, two tall decorated trees, wrapped presents and warm fairy lights. Santa — full white beard, red velvet suit with white fur trim, black belt and boots — waves at the camera, then the person next to him dances the routine in sync: a bounce, a hand behind the head, a knee lift, a hip shake, a full turn with the backs to the camera, and big smiles at the end. Santa and the hall never change. The person next to him is the whole joke, and that person is you.

## Step 2 — Upload one full-body photo

One photo is the whole input, and it has to be a **full-body, standing shot** — the clip shows both dancers from head to toe, so a waist-up photo makes the AI invent your trousers and shoes. Face clearly visible, well lit, one person per frame, crop out anyone else.

The rules that matter:

- Use photos of yourself or people who agreed to be in the video.
- For a child, only that child's parent uploads the photo, and keep the AI label when you post.
- No celebrities, no strangers, no screenshots of the original TikTok.

## Common mistakes

- **Waist-up photo.** The clip is a full-length dance; a half-body photo forces the model to guess your legs.
- **Group photo.** One person per photo — crop to the dancer.
- **Sunglasses or heavy filters.** The face is the only thing that swaps in; keep it clean and readable.
- **Posting without the sound.** The dance without the trend sound loses the joke's timing.
