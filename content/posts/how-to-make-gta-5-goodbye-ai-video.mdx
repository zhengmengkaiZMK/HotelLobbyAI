The **GTA 5 Goodbye AI trend** puts you in the farewell scene of launch week: three men styled like Michael, Franklin and Trevor on a sunset pier hand you a video-game case and the car keys, you hold back tears in a close-up, and you drive off in a pink retro sports car while they wave. You do not need CapCut, an app, or a prompt — one photo and a browser is enough. Here is the full process.

## What you need

- **One clear photo** of one adult — face clearly visible, chest up or full length, well lit, no sunglasses or hands covering the face. Your face, hair and outfit come from this photo; the three men on the pier stay as they are.
- **A browser.** The whole process runs on the [GTA 5 Goodbye AI template](/gta-5-goodbye-ai-template) page — nothing to install.
- **That is the entire list.** The scene, the trio, the pink car and the score come from the template.

Rules that keep you out of trouble: one adult per photo, never a child, no celebrities or public figures, and only photos of yourself or of adults who agreed to be in the video.

## Step 2 — Upload your photo

The generator shows one slot labeled "Your photo". Front-facing, good light, one person. The AI reads your face, hairstyle and outfit from it and keeps them in every shot — from behind in the sunset, side-on in the handshakes, front-on in the teary close-up and at the wheel.

## Step 3 — Set ratio, resolution, and length

- **Ratio:** leave it on **auto**. The source scene is a 4:3 live-action-style clip, and auto makes the framing follow it — the result plays like the trend's original posts. 16:9 and 9:16 are there if you want another shape.
- **Length:** fixed at **30 seconds** — the full farewell, from the handshake to the drive. There are no other options, and that is the point: this trend's clip is a complete scene, not a loop.
- **Resolution:** pick the tier you want; the **exact credit cost shows on the button before you generate**. Every render is paid — there are no free credits — and a failed job returns your credits.

## Step 4 — Download, add the song, and label it AI

Download the MP4 from your history when the render finishes (long clips take a few minutes). Then post it:

- Caption it for launch week — "13 years in Los Santos", "2013–2026", "goodbye GTA 5, hello GTA 6".
- Hashtags: **#gta5goodbye** and **#goodbyegta5** are the trend's home tags.
- **Add a farewell song in the TikTok, Reels, or Shorts library.** Most goodbye edits use Wiz Khalifa's "See You Again"; the MP4 keeps the reference scene's own quiet score, so layer the song over it when you post.
- **Turn on the AI-generated label.** TikTok and Instagram both offer one. The scene is fan-made AI — the three men are generated characters, not the game's actors — and labeling is part of posting it responsibly.
