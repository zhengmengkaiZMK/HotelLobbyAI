If you are making a **pimpwalk entrance** video, there are two routes: the CapCut templates that took off around 31 October 2026, or the browser generator at [ViralReel's pimpwalk entrance template](/pimpwalk-entrance-ai-template). Both work. They differ in casts, casting control, watermarking and render behavior.

## The CapCut route

The CapCut wave started on 31 October 2026 with "how to make the viral doorway entrance pimpwalk hold the door trend" tutorials — the biggest of which now sits at **442K plays**. By 3 November the two main templates stood at **8,387 and 4,694 uses**.

**How it works:** find a post using either template in TikTok, tap the template link, drop in a photo, and CapCut edits the clip for you inside the app.

**Good for:** speed and zero cost. It is the fastest way to put one face into the doorway.

**Limits:**

- You get whichever doorway the template holds — the two big templates are fixed casts.
- Most versions take one photo; casting the person who holds the door is limited, and the door-holder is half the joke.
- Exports carry the CapCut watermark unless you pay CapCut.
- Face-swap quality on the strut's low angles varies a lot — the near-crouch close-up is where cheap swaps fall apart.

[ViralReel's pimpwalk entrance template](/pimpwalk-entrance-ai-template) rebuilds the scene with an image-to-video model from your photos, in the browser.
