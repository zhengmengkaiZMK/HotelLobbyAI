## What the camera should see

The joke is in the legs: knees up on the table, then the quiet climb-down. A face-only selfie gives the model nothing to climb down with.

- Full body, or at least the knees. A passport crop is the usual reason the legs tangle.
- Relaxed. The person on the table has to look like they belong there. A coffee in hand or crossed legs beats a stiff stance.
- Face the camera enough that both eyes are visible. Sunglasses, a mask, or hair over the face make the swap harder to read.
- Even light in front of the person. A window behind them turns the face into a silhouette.
- Overdressing helps. A suit on the person who sits on the table makes the mistake land harder. That is the Cream Suit cast, the 4.7 million-play version.
- JPG, PNG, and WebP work. The generator states a minimum of 300×300.
