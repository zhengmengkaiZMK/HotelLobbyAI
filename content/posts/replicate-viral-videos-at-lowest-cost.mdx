## Step 1: Get the original video

Download the trending video you want to replicate from TikTok (any standard TikTok downloader works). For this walkthrough I'll use the Burning Bridges office-party scene — the chandelier, the steel tumbler, the swagger. You can see the exact clip our template is based on here: [party-office.mp4](/templates/party-office.mp4).

## Step 2: Have Gemini or Google AI Studio reverse-prompt it

This is the trick most people miss. Instead of trying to describe the video from memory, I drop the video into Gemini (Google AI Studio works too) and make the model break it down frame by frame with this prompt:

```text
I have provided you with a video. Please analyze the video frame by frame, break down the scenes in the video, the sequence of actions and facial expressions of the main character, the sequence of actions and facial expressions of background characters, background music, characters' lip movements and lyrics. Then present them to me in the form of structured prompts.
```

## Step 5: The counterintuitive trick — send audio, not video

MiniMax H3 technically accepts a reference video, a prompt, and images all at once. But after testing, the reference-video path produced noticeably worse results. Here's the fix that changed everything:

**Strip the audio from the trending video and send that instead.** Extract the audio track, keep it under 15 seconds, and pass it to the model as the audio reference alongside the structured prompt and your photo. The audio drives the vocals and sound style, the prompt drives the choreography, and the results jumped to a level the reference video never reached.

This exact configuration — reference audio plus tuned prompt — is what our [Burning Bridges generator](/burning-bridges-ai-template) runs under the hood.
