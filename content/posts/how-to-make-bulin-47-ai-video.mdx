## Which photos get the best results

The template sets the floor, the photo sets the ceiling. We have looked at hundreds of cases across our FAQ tickets and comments, and the pattern is remarkably stable. Here are the conclusions.

Contrast is the core of the joke — the original is a professional artist going off the dome, so the least "musician" outfit you own performs best. Suits, oversized sweaters, work uniforms, pajamas — all meme material. Picture it: a guy in pajamas freestyling under red lights while a crowd films. That absurdity is the shareability.

On the technical side, four rules. Front-facing with a clear face — the red lighting eats dark faces, so this matters more here than usual. Decent, even light, no sunglasses, no heavy filters. One person per photo, no group shots. And sharp — no blurry old photos. For Pet Freestyle, one photo of the animal is enough, cats and dogs both work, and the results are weirdly good.

One more thing: if you are using a friend's photo, ask first.
