Each cast keeps its own room, walk-in and timing. Pick the one closest to the post you want to make.

## Step 2 — Upload two full-body photos, table first

This is the whole craft:

- **Photo 1** is whoever should sit on the table. **Photo 2** is the one who walks in and shows how it is done.
- **Full body, one person per photo.** The joke is in the legs — knees up on the table, then the climb-down — so a selfie crop leaves the model guessing.
- **Relaxed poses.** The person on the table has to look like they belong there. A coffee in hand or legs crossed beats a stiff passport-style shot every time.
- **Overdressing helps.** The biggest swaps put the person on the table in a suit — the mistake lands harder when they look too nice for the furniture.
- One clear face per photo; with two faces in one photo, the model picks who sits.

## Step 3 — Generate

The page opens on **9:16** with the length fixed at **15 seconds** — the full scene from the perch to the climb-down. Read the credit cost shown before you generate, then start the render. It typically takes a few minutes; finished clips land in History, and failed jobs are refunded automatically as credits.

If the climb-down looks tangled — legs swinging, feet missing the ledge — run the same photos again. The second take usually fixes it.

## Step 4 — Post it right

- Caption it the way everyone does: **"POV: your first time in a fancy cafe"**, or **"can't take bro anywhere nice"** if you cast a friend on the table.
- Tag whoever is on the table — the tag turns a scene into a joke about someone specific.
- Add **#yourfirsttimeinafancycafe**.
- Add the trend sound — Steve Lacy's "Oh Yeah?" — from the TikTok, Reels, or Shorts library. ViralReel does not license the song.
- Turn on the **AI-generated label** TikTok and Instagram both offer.

## Troubleshooting

- **Nobody climbs down / the legs tangle** — photo was not full-body, or the pose was stiff. Retry full-body and relaxed.
- **The wrong person sits on the table** — you uploaded two faces in one photo, or swapped the photo order. Photo 1 sits, photo 2 walks in.
- **The dog looks like a blob** — headshot of a dark-furred pet. Use a bright, full-body sitting photo with the fur pattern visible against the background.
