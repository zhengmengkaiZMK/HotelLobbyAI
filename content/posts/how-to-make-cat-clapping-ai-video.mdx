Late September 2026, scrolling Instagram, people hit a short comedy clip. A person offers a black cat a treat. The cat sits up, grins, claps, covers its mouth, and reaches out for a handshake. The post is from Aleksandr Kim, under the handle @merkurianin, dated 25 September 2026. Within about a week it passed 2.5 million likes.

The caption did not need a long explanation. The clap was the joke.

## What the original clip actually does

The version that spread is an AI comedy edit, not a trained pet. The recognizable beats are short enough to read with the sound off:

1. The person is in the foreground, looking down at the floor.
2. The cat sits upright.
3. It claps its front paws.
4. It brings one paw up to its mouth.

The original edit goes further — a human grin, human-like hands, and a handshake. Most reaction posts only need the clap. That is the beat a phone template can keep without pretending to be the September 25 file.

## Why it spread

The clap works as a reaction. You do not need the setup. A green-screen cutout let one cat comment on anyone else's video. A two-photo version does something else: the person and their own cat play the beat, so the post is not another paste of the same black cat.

It moved on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and X. Vertical, short, and obvious in the first second.
