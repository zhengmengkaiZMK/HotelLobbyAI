## The song, said plainly

The trend sound is a remix of "Don't You Worry 'Bout a Thing" as sung by Tori Kelly in her role as Meena in the 2016 animated film Sing. Stevie Wonder wrote the 1974 original. The scat-like bridge is the part dancers mouth on camera. Some people search for “the ba ba ba dance from Sing.” Same sound.
