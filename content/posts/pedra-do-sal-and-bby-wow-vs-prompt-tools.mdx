## What a prompt tool makes you do instead

A general image-to-video or text-to-video tool starts from a blank box. You write something like "a person dancing in a square at night" or "a guy eating bread at a party." The model then invents a new crowd, a new hall, and a new camera. The recognizable format — the Rio square, or the four-beat bread bite — is not guaranteed, so you generate again until the shot happens to look like the trend.

That path can be the right one when you want a new scene. It is the long way around when the point is to stay inside a format people already know. This comparison does not estimate what those extra attempts cost on any other product.
