The NostalgIA trend is simple to recognize and simple to join: an AI video styled as a recreated retro TV commercial, starring you. One photo is the whole production. This is the exact workflow — no editor, no CapCut project, no prompt writing.

## What you are making

A NostalgIA AI video on [ViralReel](/) is a new 9:16 MP4 generated from your photo: the letterboxed standard-definition picture of a 2000s TV broadcast, a sunny suburban backyard, a chocolate crisp bar with an unreadable wrapper, your bite, your straight-to-camera grin, and the red outlined jingle text flashing across the lower third. It is an original parody ad — not a real commercial, not a reposted TikTok — which is what makes it safe to post with the AI label on.

Not sure what the trend is first? Read [What is NostalgIA CapCut](/blog/what-is-nostalgia-capcut).

## Step by step

1. **Open the template.** Go to the [NostalgIA CapCut template](/nostalgia-ai-template). The retro frame, the backyard set, the prop action, and the jingle text are all pre-built.
2. **Upload one photo.** A clear, front-facing shot of yourself — face toward the camera, chest up or half body. JPG, PNG, and WebP work. Skip sunglasses, group shots, and heavy filters: your face carries the whole ad. Only use a photo of yourself or an adult who agreed to it.
3. **Pick a lead role.** Five variants ship with the template: Classic Sweetheart, Blonde Bob, Long Hair, Butterfly Necklace, and Star '85. Each keeps your face and changes the character styling around it. The prompt is pre-filled and stays editable — keep the retro set and the jingle text so the result still reads as this trend.
4. **Keep 9:16 and generate.** The length is fixed at 10 seconds so the clip loops like the trend posts, and the vertical ratio is what TikTok, Reels and Shorts use. Read the credit cost the generator shows, then generate.
5. **Download the MP4** as soon as the render finishes. Paid credit packs and monthly plans export without a ViralReel watermark.
6. **Add the sound, keep the pov caption, label it AI.** In TikTok, Reels or Shorts, search the trend sound in the sound picker — the retro jingle audio people attach to the format. Keep the caption verbatim: "pov: you always wanted to know how you'd look in this ad." Turn on the AI-generated label where the app asks, and post natively — cross-posting tools add watermarks and throttle reach.

## Three tips that separate good posts from scroll-past ones

- **Match the role to the photo.** The five lead roles exist so the ad styling sits naturally on your face — pick the closest one instead of forcing the default.
- **Batch your renders.** Generate two or three roles and post them back to back; a "channel surfing" run of ads beats one clip on a trend this new.
- **Never upload what you do not have rights to.** No minors, no celebrities, no strangers, and do not pass the output off as a genuine old commercial — the AI label stays on. The format works precisely because the parody is honest.

## Does the download include the sound?

No — and that is deliberate. The MP4 carries the picture only; ViralReel does not license the trend audio. Adding the sound inside TikTok is what groups your post with the trend feed. The caption and the sound together are the format's filing system.
