Every version of the **first time in a fancy cafe** trend uses the same song: **"Oh Yeah?" by Steve Lacy**. Here is where the pairing comes from and how to add it to your own clip.

## What the song is

"Oh Yeah?" is the title track of Steve Lacy's 2026 album *Oh Yeah?* — the follow-up to *Gemini Rights*, the record behind "Bad Habit". The song also plays over the **end credits of *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*** (in cinemas from 31 July 2026), which is how a lot of people first heard it.

## Why it fits the cafe scene

The song's title line lands right as the man on the tiny table realizes what he is sitting on. A side-eyed smirk of a song under a side-eyed sideways look — the music does the commentary so the video never has to. That synchronization is why searches like "fancy cafe oh yeah" point straight at the track.

On the original @leehoustonx post from 1 November 2026, the audio reads "original sound - leehoustonx", but the underlying track is "Oh Yeah?".

## The song's own trend history

This is the song's second meme of the season. In early autumn 2026 it carried the **"Oh Yeah? City Walk"** format — steady walking shots through cities, cut on the beat, no punchline. The fancy cafe trend borrowed the track and gave it a punchline. Same song, different joke — do not confuse the two when you search.

## How to add it to your video

ViralReel does not license "Oh Yeah?" or any recording of it. Your clip keeps the reference timing, so:

1. Download your MP4 from [the fancy cafe template](/first-time-in-a-fancy-cafe-ai-template).
2. In TikTok, Reels, or Shorts, search the track name — "Oh Yeah Steve Lacy" — not the meme name.
3. Cut the song so the title line lands on the realization moment, and post with #yourfirsttimeinafancycafe.

Clips that keep the song cut on the beat read as part of the trend; clips without it read as random cafe footage.
