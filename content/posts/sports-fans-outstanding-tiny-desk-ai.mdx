## Why the sports version keeps moving

The scene is fixed, so a new cast is readable in a second. “Outstanding” is a celebration song. The funniest weeks are the ones that did not earn a celebration: a Saturday when ranked teams lost, a slump, a group chat that just watched a bad game. That contrast is why sports feeds picked the clip up, and why friend groups copied the desk after the first coaches version.
