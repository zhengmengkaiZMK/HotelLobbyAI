If your TikTok feed has turned into a parade of people arriving like they own the block, you have met **Pimpwalk Entrance** — also known as the **hold the door trend**. A friend pulls a shop door open at night, grooves beside it, and somebody comes through in a slow, low, leaned-back pimp walk, drops almost to a crouch, then rises into a close-up like the camera waited for them all night.

Short version: the scene was posted by **@mrdanielkimchi on 17 October 2026**, it is widely described as AI-generated, the audio is his "original sound", and within two weeks TikTok had run a 1970s dandy, costumed sisters, Mr Bean, Walter White and Messi through the same doorway. ## The scene, beat by beat

1. **The doorway.** Night, a small shop with red-trimmed cream walls, camera at chest height inside. The doorway is drawn — it is the same shop in every version.
2. **The door swings wide.** The friend holding it grooves in place. They are the hype man; the guest is the star.
3. **The strut.** The guest comes through in a pimp walk — knees bent, shoulders rolled back, head tilted — dropping almost to a crouch while gliding forward.
4. **The rise.** Straight up into a close-up by the camera. Main-character energy, delivered.

The pimp walk itself is much older — the exaggerated strut of 1970s films — but the 2026 version is not a dance anyone learns. It is an entrance someone else is made to do, which is why it works best on your dad, your boss, or your dog.

## It is not a real place

One of the most common confusions: people search "where is pimpwalk entrance located" and "what happened at pimpwalk entrance recently" as if it were a venue. There is no address — the doorway is drawn, part of the AI generation. "Getting in" means making your own version.
