**Wassup in a minute** is a TikTok trend from late October 2026: hand-drawn streetwear anime characters standing in a line on a plain white background, swaying side to side on the beat of a jersey-club remix. The name comes from the trend sound — most people hear the line "wassup in a minute", and the trend has been called that ever since.

It is one of the fastest-moving art trends of the month: it went from a single animator's post on 22 October 2026 to AI remakes and "upload one photo" tutorials within three days. This page explains what it is, where it came from, and how to make your own version on [ViralReel](/).

## What the trend looks like

Every wassup in a minute video shares the same four ingredients:

1. **The white background.** No set, no room, no effects — just flat white, with a soft drop shadow that moves behind each character.
2. **The streetwear anime look.** Characters drawn as Atlanta rappers: glossy puffer jackets, gold chains, baggy jeans, and — in the original — a fanned stack of banknotes in one hand. Fans call this drawing style **Atlanta Lock**, because it started as fan art of the football anime *Blue Lock* drawn as Atlanta rappers.
3. **The sway.** The only animation is a side-to-side rock, one character after another, perfectly on the beat. That minimalism is why remakes spread so fast — it is easy to recognize and easy to redo.
4. **The sound.** A jersey-club remix built on "From Time", the 2013 Drake track with Jhené Aiko. Early in the remix the vocal says "wassup, been a minute", which most listeners hear as "wassup in a minute".

## Where it came from: the timeline

- **For years before:** fans of *Blue Lock* drew its players as Atlanta rappers. The fan nickname for the style was Atlanta Lock.
- **January 2026:** the jersey-club remix of "From Time" started circulating on TikTok.
- **22 October 2026:** the US animator [@ristyisvalid](https://www.tiktok.com/tag/wassupinaminute) posted several streetwear drawings swaying side to side on a white background.
- **23 October 2026:** the follow-up post, captioned "Wassup in a minute", passed a million views within days and gave the trend its name.
- **24–25 October 2026:** fans redrew the edit with characters from other anime, and AI "upload one photo" tutorials started spreading — the version that turns *you* into the swaying character.

## Why is it spelled three ways?

The lyric is "wassup, **been** a minute". Listeners split into "wassup in a minute", "wassup been a minute" and "wassup wait a minute" — some even hear "wasala in a minute". The hashtag [#wassupinaminute](https://www.tiktok.com/tag/wassupinaminute) settled the official spelling, but all of them point at the same trend.
