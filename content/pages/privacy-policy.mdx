## Who we are

ViralReel (https://www.viralreel.app) is an independent website for making short AI videos from photos. You pick a template — including Hotel Lobby, Burning Bridges, Bulin 47, and Ozil Goal — or use the AI video generator, upload the photos the template asks for, and download the MP4.

ViralReel is a website, not a mobile app. It is not affiliated with the unrelated apps that use the name ViralReel on the App Store or Google Play, and it is not an official tool of any person, club, or brand that appears in a template.

This policy explains what we collect when you use viralreel.app, why we collect it, and who receives it. Questions go to [support@viralreel.app](mailto:support@viralreel.app).

## Information we collect

### Account

When you create an account we store the name and email address you give us, a hashed password if you sign up with email, and the account identifier returned by Google or GitHub if you use those sign-in options when they are enabled. We also store the language you use, the IP address at sign-up, and a marketing source tag when your visit includes one.

If email verification is turned on, we send a verification message to the address on the account.

### Photos, settings, and videos

To generate a video we store:

- the reference photos you upload
- the template, prompt, model, aspect ratio, resolution, duration, and audio option you choose
- the status of the job, the credit cost, and the finished video when generation succeeds

We keep these so the job can run, so you can download the result from your generation history, and so we can refund credits or answer a support request about that job.

### Credits and payments

We store your plan, credit balance, credit grants, credit usage, and subscription status. A generation shows its credit cost before it starts. Credits are deducted when the job is accepted and returned automatically if that job fails or is canceled.

Checkout is handled by the payment processor shown on the payment page, such as Stripe, Creem, or PayPal. That processor collects your card or wallet details. ViralReel receives the payment status, amount, currency, and the customer or subscription identifiers the processor sends us. We do not store your full card number.

### Device, logs, and cookies

The site runs on Cloudflare. Server logs can include your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you request, and error details. We use a session cookie to keep you signed in. We may also store a preference cookie, such as language.

If an analytics tool is enabled in our settings, that tool may set its own cookies or collect page-view data. The tools the site can use are Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Plausible, OpenPanel, and Vercel Analytics. A tool collects data only when it is switched on.

### Messages you send us

If you email [support@viralreel.app](mailto:support@viralreel.app), we keep the message and our reply so we can handle your request.

## How we use information

We use the information above to:

- create and secure your account
- generate the video you request
- show your credit balance and generation history
- charge for plans and credit top-ups, and return credits when a job fails or is canceled
- send account email, such as address verification
- detect abuse, debug failed jobs, and keep the site working
- reply to support requests
- meet legal duties

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your photos to advertise other products to you.

## AI providers that process your photos

The model you select runs outside ViralReel. When you start a generation, we send that provider the reference photos and the settings needed to make the video, including the prompt, duration, resolution, and aspect ratio. The provider returns the video or an error. Its own terms cover how it processes that material for the request.

Some models refuse a job when the photo appears to include a celebrity or a copyrighted character. In that case the job ends and the credits for that job are returned.

## Who else receives information

We share information only as needed to run ViralReel:

- **AI model providers**, to produce the video you asked for
- **Payment processors**, to take payment and manage the subscription
- **Cloudflare**, which hosts the website, the database, and the stored photos and videos
- **Email delivery**, currently Resend, when we send an account email
- **Analytics providers**, only for a tool that is enabled
- **Authorities or other parties**, when the law requires it, or when it is needed to investigate abuse, fraud, or a threat to users or to ViralReel

## How long we keep it

We keep account information, uploaded photos, generation records, and finished videos while your account is open and for as long as we need them to provide the service, handle a dispute, or meet a legal duty. Credits stop working on the expiry date attached to that grant.

You can ask us to delete your account and the photos and videos stored with it by emailing [support@viralreel.app](mailto:support@viralreel.app). We may keep a limited record where the law requires it, including a payment record, or where we need it to resolve an open dispute.

## Security

We use access controls and encrypted connections to protect account data, photos, and videos. No website can guarantee that storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Use a unique password and keep your sign-in details to yourself.

## Your choices

You can update your profile from account settings, and you can cancel a subscription from the billing page. Cancellation stops the next renewal. Access you already paid for continues until the end of that billing period.

You can block cookies in your browser. Blocking the session cookie will sign you out. You can also use your browser or the analytics provider’s own opt-out where that tool offers one.

To ask for a copy of your account data, or to ask us to correct or delete it, email [support@viralreel.app](mailto:support@viralreel.app). We may need to confirm that the request comes from the account holder.

## Children

ViralReel is not for children. You must be at least 18, or the age of majority where you live if that age is higher. Do not upload a photo of a child, and do not use the service to create sexual or exploitative content of any person.

## Where data is processed

Cloudflare and the AI and payment providers we use may process information in countries other than yours. We send them only what the service needs, as described above.
